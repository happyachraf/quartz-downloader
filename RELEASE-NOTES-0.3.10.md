# Quartz Downloader 0.3.10

## Share a Spotify song

- Share a Spotify song directly to Quartz, or paste its track link in the app.
- Open the purple download sheet with the Spotify title, artist, cover and audio options.
- Choose Original audio for the best available source stream or MP3 at 320 kbps.
- Show the available YouTube matches. A strong match is selected automatically; uncertain versions require an explicit choice.
- Keep the Spotify title, artist and cover when saving audio.
- Reuse the same queue and duplicate detection for single songs and playlists. Single-song shares leave saved playlist workspaces alone.
- Support standard and localized Spotify track URLs with share parameters.

Spotify supplies public song metadata and artwork; the downloaded audio comes from the selected YouTube match. Available recordings depend on the source and region.
